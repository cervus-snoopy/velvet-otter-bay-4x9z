A newspaper front page that sums up the world's news of **Sunday 22 November 2026** in one image.

The page carries a masthead naming the day, a lead story on the COP31 climate talks running into overtime in Antalya, second stories on the Gulf escalation and Trump's arrival in Shenzhen for APEC, and **50 dated stories in columns** from every region and field — Europe, the Middle East, the Americas, Asia, Africa, sport, tech and markets — plus a UTC timeline of the day as it unfolded and a date-check footer. Every story happened or was first reported on 22 November 2026, drawn from Reuters, BBC, AP, USA Today, the Wall Street Journal and wire reports dated that day. The image is rendered from hand-written HTML/CSS with a headless browser; no image API was used.

## Files

- `entry.png` — the front page, 1024×1024, rendered from HTML/CSS
- `main.ts` — Node 22 program that copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`

Run it with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts`.
